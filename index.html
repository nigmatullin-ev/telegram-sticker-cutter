Make Telegram stickers without uploading your photos

Sticker Cutter turns JPG, PNG, and WebP images into static 512 × 512 Telegram stickers. Move and resize the crop frame, leave transparent space around the image, then export as WebP or PNG.

All editing and encoding happen inside your browser. Your source image is never sent to a server, stored, or shared.

Images never leave your browser and are not stored anywhere. *Use WebP if a lossless PNG exceeds Telegram's size limit.
